# 12: The village square's ground is made of diamond tiles: cobbles, pavement, soil and grass, no sky

**What to build:** The parent's wish (15 Nov 2026): *no blue band at the top of the village square; ground tiles all the way up; the ground can be cobbles and pavement in some places, soil and grass in others; different kinds of cobbles and other tiles.* The square's scene (`ui/square.gd`, block `trip.square` in `rules/49-trip.json`) today is a mockup: a sky colour above a "horizon", one flat tile (`tiles/path`) stretched over the rest, and pictures standing on it. This ticket replaces that ground with **diamond-shaped (isometric 2:1) tiles painted in OpenArt**, in the style of the farm map's tiles (`assets/tiles/`), laid out as a map in the data.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** done

**Milestone:** prototype

**Size:** sitting (the picture run is most of it)

## Decided (Claude, on the parent's wish; all of it is data, so the parent can change any of it after looking)

- **Interim, done 15 Nov 2026:** `horizon` is `0.0` (no sky band: the ground reaches the top edge of the scene, with the flat `tiles/path` tile) and the sailed windmill is gone from the square's deco (the village miller's mill is **water powered**, only the farm's own mill has sails; a water-mill picture is not drawn yet, so the square has none). Everything below replaces the flat ground.
- **The tiles (pictures):** one or two OpenArt sheets (skill `openart-pictures`; the same flat-diamond-on-plain-background way the farm's ground tiles were made: look at `art-inbox/ART_LIST.csv` / `tools/art_sheets.json` for the sheet that made `assets/tiles/`) of about 3 × 4 diamonds each. Kinds: **cobbles** (at least three looks: round river cobbles, grey setts, mossy cobbles), **pavement** (two or three flagstone looks, one with a worn path through it), **edges** (cobbles meeting grass; cobbles meeting soil), **soil** (packed earth, tilled), **grass** (plain, flowers). No people, no objects, no shadows that run over the diamond's edge, the same light from the top left as the farm map. Each tile is cut to `assets/square_tiles/<kind>_<n>.png` (the importer tool as for `assets/tiles/`).
- **The ground (code):** one deep module `game/farm/ui/square_ground.gd` with one function, `build(box: Vector2, layout: Dictionary) -> Control`: it covers the scene from the top edge to the bottom with diamonds (a grid of about 8 diamonds across the scene's width; sizes are shares of the scene's **width**, never of its height), choosing a variant per cell by a stable hash of the cell (so a redraw never changes the look), and nothing else in `square.gd` knows about tiles: `_draw` calls `build` where it made the sky and the flat ground. The scene stays the same for every redraw and no tile is tappable.
- **The layout (data):** in `49-trip.json` `square.ground`: a short ASCII map (one string per row, one letter per cell: `c` cobbles, `p` pavement, `e` cobbles-to-grass edge, `s` soil, `g` grass, `f` flower grass) plus a `kinds` table (letter → the tile files of that kind). The first layout: pavement round the stalls and the board, cobbles on the lanes between, soil in the corner by the well and the bench, grass at the edges and the top corners, so the square looks lived-in, not a floor. `tools/check_data.py`: every letter of the map is a known kind, every named tile file exists, the rows are equally long.
- **Deco:** the houses, trees, well and bench stay as the deco list has them (they stand on the ground); no sailed windmill. The painted square of `pictures/04` stays the parent's alternative: if a painted backdrop file exists (`backdrop`), it still replaces everything, as the code comment says.
- **Credits:** the OpenArt budget the parent gave (2000) is spent but 20 credits (1980 used on 15 Nov 2026): one sheet version (15 credits) fits. More versions and a second sheet need the parent to raise the limit. 💡 **Recommendation: allow +120 credits for this ticket** (about 4 sheets × 2 versions). The agent makes the first sheet, looks at it, and **stops with a progress note and the credits it needs** if one version is not enough; it never goes above what the parent allowed.
