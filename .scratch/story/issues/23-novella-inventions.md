**What to build:** the parent decides which of the ten things the retelling invented stay in the story, so the compression into game pieces starts from settled material.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** needs-parent

**Milestone:** story

**Size:** short (after reading the novella; each item is a yes or no)

💡 for all ten: **yes**, except where noted. They fill gaps without breaking a locked fact.

- [ ] 1. Lark appears in chapter 1 (Longnight) and knows about a blue mitten.

- [ ] 6. Bo remembers a girl handing him an empty bucket on the fire night.

- [ ] 8. Berit's alpaca page sits in the chapter (in the game it is free play only).
- [ ] 9. Fenna's purse is spent on the antidote (the reason poison costs all coins).
- [ ] 10. Small names: the hens Agatha, Big Agatha and The Other One; the puddle Gordon; the twins' eleven minutes; Hilde's knots; Thorne's labelled grafts; Quill's worm Gerald.
