# Notes on story-retold.md (27 Oct 2026)

A rewrite of `../archive/assembly-2026-10-27/story-repaired.md` for reading, not a replacement for it. About 36,000 words (the repaired version is about 18,700). Third person, present tense, frame and six chapters as before. The reference section at the end is left out; it lives in `../archive/assembly-2026-10-27/story-repaired.md` and the bible.

## What changed

- **Scenes instead of fragments.** The repaired text was a chain of game barks. Here most beats are joined into scenes with a want, an obstacle and a turn, with more dialogue. The "Fenna wants X. Y wants Z." design phrasing is gone.
- **Lark gets real work, about two beats a chapter, each built on the mirror method from `../canon/characters/lark.md`:**
  - Ch1 (new): Longnight. He eats porridge walking the fence rail, hopping and circling, defending "chairs are finished" until she tells him to sit down. He sits; she is still standing. A private detail (the blue mitten).
  - Ch2: the crooked crate, her list "signed: you", and a parable about a lighthouse keeper. She crosses out *frost*.
  - Ch3: the goat-eaten list ("Item four: rest. Eaten."), a parable about a king who wrote *go for a walk* at the bottom of every list; the letters sorted by length (trace).
  - Ch4: the library by weight; then he enthusiastically urges her to plough the clover, drain the pond and sell the straw, and her first "No" comes out at him before she says it to Quill. Then the clover line to Quill.
  - Ch5: Mr. Mumble and "Yours", then "I'm not tired" while falling asleep on a stone (her own Mirror Crack, which she doesn't take, and then she collapses). He leaves a bundle of eleven stalks.
  - Ch6: the Broker (stops at the rest, studies a cheese), the fallow scene, standing still at the telling, the place at the supper.
  - **His one plain sentence:** "Nobody has ever set a place for me." (at the supper, after her bend).
  - "Probably fine" is dropped as a catchphrase, following the new profile.
- **Cut** (game-flavoured or one-line visitors): pizza night, tapenade, Kit and the scarf, the painter, Vega's telescope, the theatre's cheese play, the wedding cake, the school window, the kitten, the baby blanket, Cole, the bookseller, Tom's horseshoe, Ferdinand's lamp, several tech-tree margins.
- **Kept:** every clue beat in the bible's ladder, the bell grammar, the thump language, all six rhyme returns, the five silences, the rule that *Mumyo*, "go away" and blame words stay out until the garden.

## Inventions to accept or overrule

1. Lark appears in chapter 1 (Longnight) and knows about a **blue mitten**.
2. Lark's parables (lighthouse keeper, the king's list) and the eleven-stalk bundle.
3. **Lark stays for breakfast** after the Fair, and wears a new white moonstone button (the slice 06 coat idea). The frame says he still turns up "when I've forgotten to sit down" and is probably still alive. This goes past "a visit, never a presence"; it only happens at the very end.
4. **"Ask Maud about the goat" is answered** in chapter 6: the old Moonedge goat stamped one sack short on every Share Day and the Lord's man believed her; the mother wants Fenna to know there was laughing at Moonedge too. Consistent with Jan's counting goat and with Maud's goat not being the Helper.
5. The badger flattens the **mother's letters** (the asks get smaller, the letters longer).
6. Bo remembers a girl handing him an empty bucket on the fire night (from `village-arcs.md`, made explicit).
7. Fenna patches the tent with the WEIR sack corner; she sent the tent back on the barge before the Fair.
8. **Berit's alpaca page is in the chapter** after the alpaca comes home ("I was busy."). In the game it is free play only. Drop it if Berit's guilt should stay out of the read-through.
9. Fenna's purse is spent on the antidote (gives a reason for "poison costs all coins").
10. Small names and business: the hens Agatha, Big Agatha and The Other One; the puddle Gordon; the twins' eleven minutes; Hilde's knots; Thorne's labelled grafts; Quill counting worms called Gerald.
