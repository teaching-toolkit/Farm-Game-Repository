# The novella is the current story; the game pieces will be compressed from it

On 27 Oct 2026 the parent decided that the game texts take a back seat: the master outline is the spine, the bible is canon, the
slices supply the detail. The 36,000-word retelling `novella/story-retold.md` is the current novella, written for the developer to
judge whether the story grips. Later it is compressed into new in-game pieces that **replace every current piece**; the player
uncovers them, and their order within a chapter may vary with what the player does first. Until then the 63 shipped pieces stay as
stand-ins and the 166 staged pieces stay on file, unbuilt (`I15` is parked, milestone `story`).

## Considered options

Building the 166 staged pieces first (I15) was rejected: they predate the outline run and would be replaced anyway.
