# Review of slice 06: Lark, Mumyo's traces, the melody, the forest and the awe

Fresh-eye reviewer, 23 Oct 2026. Read: charter, DECISIONS, digest and node map, the dossier (all 745 lines) and ticket 06, `characters/lark.md`, `mumyo.md`, `world/shell-and-magic.md`, the premise (melody lines), slices 01 (Revision 2, 2.3, Handed over), 02 (seeds, tune route), 03 (Revision 2, 4.3, 4.4, 5.2), 04 (dossier, `telling.md`, `night-account.md`), the QUESTIONS blocks of 04 and 07, the shipped pieces the dossier quotes (read from `data/farm/story/`, read only), the node ids it hangs seeds on, and live-wire-style `diagnose.md` and `texture.md` (wonder). Nothing was edited. Diagnose lens: presence, then bottleneck, then charge audit, then leaks.

---

## 1. Verdict in five lines

1. **Strong core, not yet mergeable.** The one idea (a gap someone leaves for you; the farmer steps over each one) is the best organising thought in the run so far, and the melody design keeps the deepest layer out of every sketch line.
2. **Bottleneck: collisions, not quality.** Slice 06 was written beside slice 04 and keeps three things 04 replaced (the Fair-time *tink*, the unchanged `c6_ajar`, the "Mum—" slip). The garden and the supper are now the most overloaded places in the book.
3. **The awe plan holds only inside this slice.** Merged with 02, 03 and 04 there are about seven awe-tails in the book, and two of the four "counted" awes (the Wall, the garden) sit on optional nodes.
4. **Lark breaks his own rules** (one visit per chapter, barks only as sorting traces) in his own joke list; about half the new jokes are proverbs, not gags. The melody and the traces depend on sound and pictures the engine does not have, and almost no seed gives a text-only version.
5. **Verdict: revise, then merge.** Fixes 1 to 6 below are needed before integration; the rest can ride with 08a/08c.

## 2. What holds (must not be lost)

- **The one idea** (rest, invitation, second clause) and the fact that it never appears in a text. Keep it as an architects' note only.
- **A = the Home phrase with words** (rulings 5 and 12). This gets the 🔒🔒 layer right: the ending the farmer hurries toward was in her own kitchen song and in the valley's cradles all along. Nothing is added to her; she stops skipping. Do not weaken this by making Home something she earns.
- **The Rest Gag** (E-1.M04): hens, goat, bunny stop at the same place and she hums on. It works literally for a six-year-old and structurally for an eight-year-old, and it never names the rest.
- **Mumyo's ladder as a conversation** (§2.1) with rule 14 (invite, offer, apologise) and rule 5 (refused: smaller and farther; accepted: bolder). It makes the traces a cause chain instead of a list, and the farmer's only visible change is *stepping round* (a bump left in a roof), never a sentence.
- **The thumb-sized hammer and the roof bump** (E-2.M06) and **the weeds in a bad smile** (E-2.M07): physical, funny, harmless, one easy yes each. Best answer to C8.
- **Mumyo drinks Maud's bucket; the thimble beside her bucket at the fire.** The empty bucket of the barn night answered by a thimble at a time is the quiet, wordless centre of the thread. Keep it (but see fix 1 for where it sits).
- **The Broker whistling Rise and Turn and stopping at the rest to study a cheese** (E-6.M24, minus the slip). Funny and mysterious, and the village's Home stays "not his to give".
- **Lark's anatomy** (§1.2): arrives without a sound, sits lower than she stands, one pointless thing, one true thing, leaves while she looks at the thing. The **echo rule** (said plainly later by someone who never heard him, done last by the farmer) is a real craft device. **"Badly hummed" / "Yours."** is the parent's line and the slice treats it right.
- **The moonstone button on the twig buttonhole**, never mentioned; **"Probably fine" as a running snag** whose third and biggest use is the goat and the ribbon.
- **The glimpse ritual** (a different part each time, never the face, never at the viewer, the farmer looking away; "did you see?" at read-aloud). Keep the idea; see fix 9 for the numbers.
- **Awe rules** (§5.1, eight rules) match the wonder register in `texture.md`, and §5.2 "held back" is wise (no shell, no full-face picture, no second wonder after the conch).
- **Rule 7, no light near anything dry** (the garden is lit by flowers on wet ground). It is also the first rule that makes Mumyo's guilt visible without a word.
- The forest as layers (edge, Line, Outer Wood, Deep, Shell) with "the Deep will not be hurried" as the reason she never gets closer. It lets the world, not her failing, own the distance.

## 3. Findings by check

These say the true thing outright; a six-year-old gets neither the joke nor the point, and an eight-year-old hears a wise man. They are the "Lark explains" failure the character sheet warns against.
- **Do the 17 sketches differ or repeat one move?** The *formula* (gag clause then true clause) is the same in nearly all; what differs is the gag type (physical stacking, reading over a shoulder, catalogue, bargaining, one word), which saves it. But the count is inflated: J1, J6, J7, J13 (part), J16, J17 are shipped; J14 is a picture; J15 is not a joke. Genuinely new jokes: nine (J2, J3, J4, J5, J8, J9, J10/J11 as one scene, J12). Yet J4, J5 and J9 are marked *bark* with Lark speaking; chapter 3 would carry M10 + J4 + J5; chapter 4 already has **two shipped visits** (`c4_no`, `c4_library`) plus J8; chapter 5 has M21 + J9 + slice 04's E-5.B25 + 04's barrel gag. ### 3.3 The melody and the 🔒🔒 layer

**Banned-word checklist, run on every proposed player-facing line and sketch** (words: enough, whole, wholeness, complete, worth, worthy, deserve, perfect, belong, right (as in all right), safe, already home, buddha, nature, plus "plenty"):

| Where | Result |
|---|---|

| E-6.M33 "she wants the supper perfect" | "perfect" appears in the *want* line, not the text; but it is the same piece (`c6_supper`) as the song; keep it out of the page text |
| "Home" as the label of the fourth phrase | design/sound-cue label only; must not become a word in any melody scene or cue description shown to a writer of text; the game already says "comes home" for Helpers |
| "Small is plenty" (Berit) | allowed by the dossier; keep it away from every melody scene |
| Dossier's own prose: "complete", "whole table", "small enough" | architects' prose; no leak |

**Result: no banned word in the proposed text.** Three near-misses need a decision:

1. **E-5.M20 second clause, "and neither, today, will you."** It tells her she is not being hurried: the meaning of the rest said aloud, in a wonder passage where the narrator should not be clever. Cut it (the dossier already marks it ❓). "Some things are for later" is shipped and enough.
2. **"Better."** A grade on a performance is the nearest the plan comes to the opposite of the layer: the melody becomes something you hum well or badly, earned by finally sitting. Keep Lark's one plain word if the parent wants it, but make it about the *sound*, not her: for example he says nothing and puts down the thing he was sorting, or the plain word is a place-word. Test with a child; ask the parent (it is the one place the 🔒🔒 layer touches a spoken line).
3. **The arc "loop, never lands, then lands with the village" can read as incomplete-then-complete.** The A = Home idea answers this (she always had it); make the supper show it as her *joining a thing already whole* (the village already sings; she stops hurrying), never as her completing the tune. The E-6.M29 note "Home lands" is fine in a composer's note, dangerous in a scene.

- **"The rest she hurries over": clear to a child, or too clever?** The *gag* is clear (the animals stop, she does not). The *concept* is too clever to be put into any text, and the dossier correctly never does. The danger is the opposite: the melody layer lives almost entirely in sound. With no hook, the text-only player gets: the stones in ch1, the Rest Gag barks, "Yours.", "You know the song", and nothing for the Wait or the Home. **Write the text-only version explicitly** (what survives with no sound) and test whether the supper still lands on its text and its pacing alone.
- **Rule 4 says the Helpers go still; slice 03's alpaca hums the alarm note** and slice 04's alpaca "hums the long note" at the lantern. The dossier does not say whether the alpaca's note is related to the Wait. Say "unrelated, her own note" or the player will hear a rhyme.
- **Tune A / tune B consistency.** With 01 (A "the song", B "the tune"; A with words, B wordless), 03 (A one note per return, sixth completes it, supper = the rhyme to A) and 04 (Berit's notes never mention a tune): consistent. Two soft spots: (a) slice 01's old you hums *without noticing* ("You're humming." "Am I?"), the dossier's old you *waits with a finger lifted*, a deliberate act. Make it an arc: frames 0 and 1 she hums without knowing (01), from frame 2 she waits. (b) "The player hears the melody incomplete eleven times and complete once" is true of B but not of the Home: A *is* the Home and is sung at every return, so the player has heard the ending since `c1_book`. That is fine (and lovely) but the claim should be rewritten.
- **Minena hums the Home right in frame 6** pre-empts the conch story in *Minena 01*. Mark it ❓ (it is in QUESTIONS only as the hum, not as the recognition).
