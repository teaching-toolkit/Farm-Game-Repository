# People of Hazelwood: a core of 20, about 30 texture names, and a cut list

Slice 02 (revision 2), companion to `dossier.md`. First written 23 Oct 2026; **triaged 23 Oct 2026 after the review** (`reviews/02-lived-in-hazelwood.md` F4, section 5) and the manager's ruling (`slices/DECISIONS.md`, "after review of slice 02"). Labels: ✅ decided · 💡 proposed (a name or fact with no source tag is 💡) · ❓ open · 🔒 hidden. Ages are for the farmer's time (Fenna is 15; Minena's grown-ups are not born yet, ✅ `characters/hazelwood-people.md`).

## 0. The rules this file now keeps

1. **Core (20): only these may carry a box** (a story piece with a line of their own). Master Quill and Lord Wexley are also core but belong to tickets 05 and 07.
2. **Texture (about 30): a name and one line, never a piece of their own.** A texture name may appear in a bark or as one line inside a core person's scene.
3. **Cut or merged:** removed from play. The list is at the end so no one reinvents them; they may live in trader data and the bible, never in text.
4. **New names per chapter: at most three** beyond the favour-givers the game itself forces on the player (the favour cards name them). Table in §6.
5. **Places a child hears: at most ten** (Moonedge, the Square, the Hall, the Mill, the Hop, the Landing, the Hurst, the forest edge with the Ribbon Oak and the Stone Line, Weir, the next valley).
6. Nobody here is cruel. People who grumble have reasons.
