# The six Helpers (and the squirrel)

Round 3, draft 1 (7 Oct 2026). Background (why each left, when each returns, the rhyme): `../world/helpers-and-rhyme.md`. This file is
about **how they are on the page**. ✅ **The Helpers do not talk** (rule 10, parent, 8 Oct 2026): they bleat, snort, squeak, nod,
stare. The farmer (and so the player) gets the idea from a look, a nod, or *how* the sound sounds, and the text says what you take it to
mean. Their comedy is in what they **do**.

All six are clever, older than they look, and **remember the farm as it was**. Each knows something about the fire. None says it outright.

## 🐰 Bunny (default Pip) · chapter 1

- **Looks:** small, brown, one ear that will not stand up. Nose never still.
- **Spine:** notice everything, panic politely. (Thumps a foot, freezes, thumps again, very sorry about the fuss: *there is a hawk.*)
- **Funny because:** sees danger in a teapot, plans escape routes from the porridge, hops out of every plan at the worst moment.
- **Warm when:** the reveal that she **kept the one patch alive** for seven years, waiting. 💡 She also nibbled every grain off the
  wheat stalk but one (she could not help it), and left that one for you. ✅ The player does not know this at first; it becomes clear when she comes back. She is the first thing you have to feed.
- **Line in the rhyme:** *Long ears sow the clover green.*
- **Job (Round 4):** weeds and clover.
- **What she knows about the fire 🔒:** she saw the lights in the barn. When a lantern is lit near the hay she thumps and will not stop.

## 🐢 Tortoise (default Mossy) · chapter 2

- **Looks:** moss on the shell, ancient eyes, small round spectacles of nothing (just the look).
- **Spine:** nothing is worth hurrying for.
- **Funny because:** everything she does takes as long as her walks. You finish a whole job between one blink of hers and the next.
- **Warm when:** the farmer sits and waits with her for the pond to fill. The tortoise says nothing at all. That is the compliment.
- **Line:** *Slow shell keeps the water clean.* **Job:** water.
- **🔒 Knows:** that the pond was drained in a hurry. She sits on the exact spot where the spring used to rise until you dig there.

## 🐐 Goat (default Bramble) · chapter 3

- **Looks:** shaggy, one horn a bit bent, a beard full of burrs. Eyes like a schemer.
- **Spine:** everything is food until proven otherwise.
- **Funny because:** eats the plan, the hat, the list, a corner of the book (Berit: *Goats lie.*). Denies all of it with her mouth full.
- **Warm when:** she eats the thistles nobody else could, and the farmer lets her (the first help accepted ✅).
- **Line:** *Brave goat clears the bramble bed.* **Job:** scrub, brambles, compost.
- **🔒 Knows:** who used to hide in the brambles and giggle. (She ate his hiding place.)

## 🐴 Pony (default Hazel) · chapter 4

- **Looks:** hazel-brown, a mane she arranges in the pond's reflection.
- **Spine:** be magnificent, but carry anything.
- **Funny because:** poses before every load, stops for admirers, does twice the work and wants it noticed.
- **Warm when:** she carries the farmer's first big order down to the village and **both of them are seen**. She was worked hardest of all,
  once. 🔒 She came back anyway.
- **Line:** *Strong pony brings the world its bread.* **Job:** carrying and selling.

## 🦡 Badger (💡 default Silverpaw) · chapter 5 (new)

- **Looks:** old, grey-striped, round spectacles (real ones). Smells of dried herbs. ✅ the badger fits the medicine chapter; 💡 he is the
  same Silverpaw who appears in *The Forest Within*.
- **Spine:** examine it first; tea first.
- **Funny because:** will not be rushed into anything, least of all an emergency. Makes tea while the kettle is still cold.
- **Warm when:** he makes the farmer sit and drink the tea. She does. (The low point and its first comfort, if the outline keeps it.)
- **Line:** *Wise badger heals what aches and stings.* **Job:** medicine (collecting, drying, mixing).
- **🔒 Knows:** he left after the children's fever, when nobody would let them rest. The farmer was one of those children.

## 🦙 Alpaca (default Alfie) · chapter 6

- **Looks:** cream-coloured, enormous eyelashes, tremendous dignity.
- **Spine:** guard the herd, politely, with spit.
- **Funny because:** unfailingly polite until something threatens the flock, and then spits with total precision. Apologises after.
- **Warm when:** the reveal that **her alarm call** woke everyone on the night of the fire. Nobody was hurt because of her. ✅ she returns last.
- **Line:** *Soft alpaca guards all things.* **Job:** guarding (pests) and wool.

## The squirrel (Nutmeg in *The Forest Within*) ✅ may cross

- Plump, cheeks always full. Hides acorns, and the **seven golden acorns** of the game are his.
- ✅ Does not talk in the normal world either. His *Forest Within* catchphrases (*"I have a SYSTEM!"*, *"I meant to do that!"*) become
  behaviour: acorns sorted into baffling rows, a fall followed by a very dignified pose, as if it was planned.
- 💡 His name comes from **Lark** in chapter 6: "Nutmeg. Obviously." (Lark knows everybody.)
- Not a Helper; a neighbour from the forest, and a bit of comic relief whenever an acorn turns up.

## Inventions in this file 💡

Looks, lines, "what they know", the badger as Silverpaw, the squirrel's name moment.
