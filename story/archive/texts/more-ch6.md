## c6_board · discovery · The board, last time
*When:* `chapter:6 + sidequests>=12` · *Register:* W→G · *Move:* Echo

The board in the square is crowded now with notices in a dozen hands. Near the bottom is the old one, the lost glove. It is still there. Nobody ever claimed it. You find this very funny.

## c6_terraces · moment · The swing, occupied
*When:* `done:orchard_2` · *Register:* W · *Move:* Echo

Page 1. The second orchard goes in, row on row, a green stair up the hill. Down in the first orchard, the old swing is still moving, just a little, as if somebody has only just got off.

Page 2. On the plank lies an apple core, eaten down to the pips. You do not need to know who. The knot holds.

## c6_seed_library · moment · A shelf of small tomorrows
*When:* `done:seed_library` · *Register:* W · *Move:* Reward

Rows of tiny envelopes, each with a name in a different hand. Carrot. Bean. “Maud's good pumpkin.” “Do not lose.” They rustle faintly when you walk past. A shelf full of next summer, put in order. You find yourself walking quietly, the way you would in front of sleeping people.

## c6_oven · moment · The big oven
*When:* `done:oven_3` · *Register:* W→G · *Move:* Reward

The big oven takes six loaves at once and rises to the occasion. The whole lane smells of it. A queue forms that nobody organised. Hilde is in it, pretending to be in the way. Old Jan is behind her, very much not pretending.

## c6_whirligig · moment · The singing pole
*When:* `done:wind_pump` · *Register:* G · *Move:* Reward

The wind pump spins. The pole sings a small, silly song, and the water comes up out of the ground as though it had been waiting for its turn. You stand there with your hands on your hips, listening. From the coop, the hens give an opinion about the noise. It is not kind.

## c6_dye · moment · The blue vat
*When:* `done:dye_vat` · *Register:* A · *Move:* Reward

You lift the cloth out of the vat, and it is green, an ugly, wet, mud-sort-of green. Then the air touches it, and it turns blue. Slowly. Like a thought arriving. You hold it, dripping, and watch it become the colour of a sky that has been waiting its turn.

## c6_theatre · scene · The cheese play
*When:* `done:sq_theatre_cheese` · *Register:* G · *Move:* Reward

The travelling theatre's play is about a cheese. It is a great cheese. It gets lost, and found, and mistaken for the moon. The lead actor holds your cheese wheel very still for the whole second act. You have never seen anyone so serious about a cheese.

## c6_agnes · scene · Thyme
*When:* `done:sq_elders_feast` · *Register:* W · *Move:* Echo

Elder Agnes sprinkles the thyme over your tapenade as though dealing cards. “Your grandmother put this in everything,” she says. “She had a saying. 'If in doubt, thyme.'” You wait for the rest. There is no rest. That was the whole saying. Somehow it is enough to keep.

## c6_flags · moment · Bunting
*When:* `done:sq_festival_flags` · *Register:* G · *Move:* Reward

The flags go up between the houses, a hundred small triangles, red and yellow and blue and one that is nobody's colour. The wind finds them and they all lean the same way at once. It makes the whole square look as if it were about to say something good.

## c6_orphanage · scene · Jam and a spoon
*When:* `done:sq_orphanage_jam` · *Register:* W · *Move:* Reward

At the long table by the orphanage window, the jam goes round on thick slices of your bread. Nobody speaks, because everyone's mouth is full. One small child puts the empty jar to her ear, as though a little of the summer might still be in there. Matron Elsie pretends not to see.

## c6_well · moment · The village well
*When:* `done:sq_village_well` · *Register:* W · *Move:* Reward

The well stands finished, a ring of grey stone, and the first bucket comes up on its rope. The water is so clear you can see your own face in it. Behind your face, a row of other faces, leaning in. All of Hazelwood is trying to look into the same hole at once.

## c6_telescope · scene · Stars
*When:* `done:sq_telescope` · *Register:* A · *Move:* Echo

The astronomer at the telescope waves you over. “Look,” Vega says, and steps back. At first, black. Then a smear of silver, and then it is not a smear. It is hundreds. You say nothing. After a long time Vega says, quietly, “Yes. That is what I say, too.”

## c6_poet · scene · The scarf
*When:* `done:sq_poet_scarf` · *Register:* G→W · *Move:* Reward

Page 1. Kit wraps the rainbow scarf three times round and declares it “every colour of every thought I almost had.”

Page 2. Then Kit writes it down. Then Kit reads it aloud, to you, to the cow, and to a passing man who was only asking the way. He says it is very good, and that the way is left.

## c6_painter · scene · Saffron
*When:* `done:sq_painter_saffron` · *Register:* W · *Move:* Reward

The painter mixes the saffron into a little pot, and the pot goes gold. She paints one stroke across the paper, and a field is suddenly there, in the afternoon, with the light on it. “Thank you,” she says. She looks as if she means for the whole season.

## c6_quilt · moment · A patchwork
*When:* `done:linen_bedding` · *Register:* W · *Move:* Omen · *Priority:* 65

Spread across the linen on your bed, a patchwork cover, every square a different cloth. You can pick out a few: Maud's checked apron, a scrap of blue, something that smells faintly of lavender. All of Hazelwood, sewn together by people you have not quite caught doing it.

## c6_eve · moment · The fire, again
*When:* `seen:c6_bend + after:c6_bend:3` · *Register:* W→A · *Move:* Echo · *Priority:* 80

Page 1. Evening. You light the fire in the yard, the first thing you ever lit here. The spark takes on the first try. The ash around it glows a little, as if it remembers something.

Page 2. You sit with your back against the fence you built. The bucket, by the door, is full of rain. None of this was here when you came. Now you are sitting in the middle of it.
