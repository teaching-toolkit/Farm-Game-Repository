# Hazelwood Harvest: the story

The story told through the farm game: Fenna's return to the burned farm, the six Helpers and the village. This glossary holds the
words used to write, plan and deliver that story. Canon facts live in `canon/story-bible.md`; decisions in `docs/adr/`.

## Language

### People and beings

**The farmer**:
The player's character as the player meets her: addressed as "you", named by the player, never described.
_Avoid_: the girl, the heroine (in player text)

**Fenna**:
The farmer's name for the writers: female, 15 at the start, Minena's grandmother. Never used in player text unless the player keeps it.

**Minena**:
The grandchild who hears the story at Fenna's bedside, decades later.

**Helper**:
One of the six animals who left after the fire and return one per chapter (bunny, tortoise, goat, pony, badger, alpaca). They do not talk. In the game they are the pets.
_Avoid_: pet (in story text), companion

**Mumyo**:
The hand-sized imp with bells who was the girl's only playmate; present only as traces.
_Avoid_: the imp (in player text before the telling)

**Lark**:
The practically immortal wanderer who says true things as jokes; one visit per chapter.

**The Lord**:
Lord Wexley, who takes the share and owes someone above him; never seen.

### Shape of the story

**Frame**:
A bedside scene with the old farmer and the grandchildren; seven of them, between the chapters, never inside one.

**Chapter**:
One of six parts of the story, each ending with a Helper's return; the same chapters as the game's.

**The telling**:
The scene in chapter 6's night garden where the barn night is told for the first time.

**The bend**:
The moment in chapter 6 when the farmer says aloud what she saw.

**Awe**:
A rare moment of wonder; five in the game.

**Silence**:
One of the five things the book never says.

**Clue ladder**:
The questions of the barn mystery, each planted, hinted, then confirmed.

### The writing and its stages

**Piece**:
One unit of story the game shows: a box with text and maybe a picture, a speech bubble, a letter or a frame scene.
_Avoid_: text, scene (for game units)

**Shipped piece**:
A piece built into the game (63 today, stand-ins until the novella is compressed).

**Staged piece**:
A piece written and on file but not built (166, parked).

**Bark**:
A one-line reaction, shown as a speech bubble and tapped away.

**Novella**:
The prose retelling of the whole story for reading (`novella/story-retold.md`); the source the new pieces will be compressed from.

**Story bible**:
The one canon file; it wins over every other story file.

**Master outline**:
The chapter-by-chapter spine of the story (`outline/master/`).

**Seed** (of the registry):
One numbered story idea in the outline's registry.
_Avoid_: idea, beat (when the registry entry is meant)

**Slice**:
One themed dossier from the outline run of 23–24 Oct 2026 (now archived).

**Data wish**:
A change to the game data or engine that the story asks for; built only once the parent says yes.

**Text-change request**:
A proposed edit to a shipped or staged piece, from the outline run.
