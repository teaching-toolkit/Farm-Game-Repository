## Where things are 💡

The game's map already puts the **forest in from the top left** and the **village road from the top right**. The world matches it.

- **Moonedge** (💡 name; the farm): on the edge of the Moonstone Forest, on a gentle hillside with a stream below.
- **The Moonstone Forest:** to the west and north. Large white stones scattered through it, some as big as houses. The conch lies deep
  inside, many hours' walk. Fenna is not allowed in alone as a child.
- **Hazelwood:** a half hour down the road. Hazel groves all round. The market square with a well, a notice board, the bakery.
- **The Hazelwood manor:** on the hill above the village. The Lord lives here.
- **The Hazel stream:** runs from the forest past the farm and the village. The village mill is on it.
- **Weir:** the river town downriver, several days away. Where Fenna's parents live.
- **The next valley:** **Wren the healer**, the herb woman, lives there (the game's chapter-3 tree-lined road brings her; she is also in two favours).
- **The coast:** far; flax seed comes from there.

## Then (Fenna's time, Y+7) and now (Minena's time, about 55 years on) 💡

| | Then | Now |
|---|---|---|
| The road | A muddy track with potholes; only a seed seller on foot | Cobbled, tree-lined, a stone bridge |
| The village | Small and tired; the Lord's rising shares squeezed it too. The market is thin. | Ivy, flowers, cobbles, busy; "everyone is friends" |
| The manor | A stiff Lord, a ledger-keeping steward | Quiet; the family went elsewhere (❓ later) |
| The farm | A burnt ruin | Moonedge Farm, run by one of Fenna's children |
| The forest | A place children are told to keep out of | Still mysterious; Minena goes in; the conch calls |

**The nice consequence:** the game's road tiers (muddy track → brushwood → potholes filled → gravel → kerbstones → cobbles → trees →
paved road and bridge) are the road *to Hazelwood*. As the player builds the road, they are building the Hazelwood that Minena
later knows. The village prospers because the farm does, and merchants and travellers come because the road is good. That is the
village strand (`../00-premise.md` §5) made visible on the map.

## How the village feels about Fenna 💡

- **They remember a thin girl always carrying something**, a farm that burnt, and a family that left in a hurry. Most were sorry.
  Some blamed the family (the Lord said they were careless). A few still do.
- **Hazelwood needs Moonedge.** Before the slide, the farm gave the village flour, wool, milk and honey. Its ruin hurt Hazelwood
  too. So the village's welcome is real once it trusts she means to stay.
- **She earns her way in through food and work**, not talk (her spine). A dish is how she says hello.

## Who they are

Minena's grown-ups (Mrs. Appleby, Mr. Odinson, Mrs. Solemate, Irian) are about forty in her time, so **in Fenna's time they are not
born yet**. Their **families' shops** are already there: the Appleby bakery, the Odinson smithy, the Solemate cobbler's. Fenna's
contemporaries are in their late sixties in Minena's time, like **Mr. Greenstem**. Full list: `../characters/hazelwood-people.md`.
