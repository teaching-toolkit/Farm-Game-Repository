## Game time ✅ (D11)

The game's clock is the Time Quiz (a season is 30 questions), so a full playthrough is nine to twelve game years if anyone counted (one bot run: 8.9; the project log's full playthrough of about 1,417 answers: about 12).
**Nobody counts.** No in-game text gives a year or Fenna's age after the opening; winters simply come round. The timeline below
dates only the backstory and the frame.

## The fire

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Y0, late summer** | The fourth dry month. The hay is tinder. At dusk Fenna (8) slips into the hay barn to play with Mumyo, the only play she gets. His dancing lights; a lantern tips over. She runs out shouting. The **alpaca's alarm call** wakes the whole household; nobody is hurt. House, barn and sheds burn. Mumyo runs back into the shell. The alpaca leaves at dawn. **The last Helper is gone.** |

## After the fire

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Y0 + weeks | The Lord ends the lease and 💡 makes sure no one in his lands will take the family on. Moonedge stands empty. |
| Y0 onward | 🔒 Mumyo does not go far. He hides at the forest edge near the ruins, ashamed, and starts the night garden in the marsh that was once the River Meadow. The bunny, too, stays close and keeps one patch alive. |
| Y0 + months | The family leaves the district. The parents, the younger siblings and the older cousin go to the river town of **Weir** (💡 name), several days away. **Fenna and Berit go to a small hill cottage** (✅ option 3). Berit raises Fenna there, from memory, with the book. |
| Y+1 to Y+7 | Fenna grows up on a hill with her grandmother. She sees her parents in the town only now and then. The cousin says she was seen near the barn. She says she saw an imp. Nobody believes a child. In time even she is not sure. |
| Y+7, winter | Berit dies. The box is left to Fenna: the farm book, a tin pot and a dented bucket. Fenna is to move to her parents in the town after the funeral. |
| Y+7, early spring | The melody starts. Fenna asks for "a few weeks" before the town, takes the box and a borrowed tent and walks alone to the ruins of Moonedge. On the one patch that is still good she finds a single ripe wheat stalk, every grain nibbled off but one ✅: **the one seed**. **THE GAME STARTS.** |
