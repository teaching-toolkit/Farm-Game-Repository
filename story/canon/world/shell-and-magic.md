## The shell ✅

The Moonstone Forest houses a mysterious **shell**, a giant white conch half-sunk in mossy ground, taller than a house, deep inside the
forest. It is a **threshold**: on the other side lies the realm where the magical beings live (the **Forest Within**: fairies, a
tree spirit, a talking mushroom and so on, as in the parent's other stories). In this game **the shell is never entered**; the
game stays on the near side. Minena goes through it in her own story, *Minena 01*, fifty-odd years later.

## Who crosses, and who does not ✅ (with 💡 details)

| Who | Crosses into the normal world? | How |
|---|---|---|
| The six **Helpers** | Yes | By the Bargain; they live at the farm as clever animals |
| **Mumyo** (imp) | Yes | Appears and disappears at will; hand-sized |
| **Lark** | Yes, and always has | Immortal wanderer; does not need the shell ✅ |
| **The squirrel** (Nutmeg in the Forest Within) | Yes ✅ | A plump squirrel who hides acorns; the golden acorns are his. 💡 Unnamed until chapter 6. |
| **The magpie** (Mara) | **No** ✅ | Stays inside. Pests on the farm are ordinary birds. |
| Fairies, dryads, tree spirits, trolls and the rest | No | They stay inside the shell |

## The rules 💡

1. **Magic is small and sideways.** It never does the farmer's work for her. It nudges: a bell, a light, a hint, a returned animal.
2. **The Helpers have a job and a limit.** They help with what the people do *with* them. They cannot do what the people refuse to do
   or stop. They leave when the Bargain is broken and return only when the farm shows it has learned.
3. **The Bargain:** the Helpers help; the people take only what the land can give back. Said once, in the book, as the rhyme.
4. **Helpers look like animals,** just too clever. Children notice first. Adults say "what a sensible goat."
5. **Mumyo's pranks are harmless.** Things are moved, never lost; nobody is hurt (the fire is the one time it went wrong, and he has
   never forgiven himself). His bells ring softly when he feels something. ✅ **Fenna never sees him again after the fire**; the
   player sometimes glimpses him in a story picture (💡).
6. **Lark never solves anything.** He says true things as jokes. Nobody can be cross at a joke.
7. **Moonstones** are said to have fallen from the sky, gifts from the moon. They hum very faintly if you put a hand on them. They
   mark the old boundary between the ordinary world and the shell. Lots of them lie near Moonedge, in the rocks of the first field.
8. **The melody** comes from the conch ✅ (see below).
9. **Time** in the shell: unspecified; not needed for this game.
10. ✅ **The Helpers do not talk** (parent, 8 Oct 2026). They bleat, snort, squeak, nod, stare. The farmer, and through the farmer
    the player, understands them from a nod, a strange look, or how the sound sounds. Grown-ups think the farmer talks to the animals,
    as farm children do. The squirrel follows the same rule. Mumyo, a creature of the shell, can be heard by anyone (his bells) but is
    seen only when he wants. Talk in the texts comes from people (villagers, Lark, the frame), so the comic exchanges with the Helpers
    are physical: what they do, and what you take it to mean.
11. ✅ **Belief, lightly worn** (parent, 8 Oct 2026). A pagan-flavoured world **without religion**: no church, chapel, priest or
    prayer. People believe in spirits, fairies, dwarves and the like the way they believe in weather: a bowl of milk for the house
    spirit, a word to the well, a ribbon on the old oak. Nobody is fervent about it; they are busy working, gossiping and looking
    after each other.

## The melody ✅ and the deepest layer 🔒🔒

The melody comes from the conch. The deepest layer (never in any text, only in the shape of things): it is the **listener's own
wholeness projected into the world**: the part of a person that already knows she is enough. The conch plays it back to her. It
calls whoever most needs it: Fenna at 15, who cannot stop working, and Minena at 7, who cannot stop proving herself.
In the game it is only a tune: the player hears it faintly in the music at certain moments (💡 a soft solo phrase when each Helper returns). ✅ In the medicine chapter (chapter 5 ✅), while gathering in the forest, the farmer sees the conch from afar, and does not go closer.

## What Lark knows 🔒

Everything about the shell, the Bargain, the fire and the melody. He tells it only as jokes, and he never pushes. His reason (💡):
he has watched people be told the truth too early and be wrecked by it. Wit-like: sharp, light, kind underneath.

## The imp, as a person 🔒

Mumyo is real. He is also, as the parent put it, what a child's suppressed need for mischief looks like from outside. Both are true; the
story does not choose. For Fenna (who was never sure) both are the answer.
