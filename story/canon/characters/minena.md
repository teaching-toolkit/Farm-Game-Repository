## In the frame

✅ (parent, 8 Oct 2026) The frame is **grandchildren asking *you* for a story**; Minena is one of them, the one the frame follows. They
call you *you* (du), never Grandma or Grandpa. 💡

- She asks the questions the player is asking ("Did the farm really burn down? Was there really an imp?").
- She wants an answer *now*; Fenna takes her time. That is the frame's small comedy.
- She hears tiny bells at the window that you do not.
- Her spine echoes the farmer's: **be the helpful one, so they'll like me.** Neither of them says so. The deepest layer: both hear the conch.

## The last scene ✅

*"If you meet a little fellow with bells, tell him I was never angry."* Minena promises. That night she dreams of a melody, which is where *Minena 01*
begins. The promise is what she carries into the shell.
