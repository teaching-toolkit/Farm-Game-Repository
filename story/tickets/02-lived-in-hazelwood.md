# 02: Slice 2: a year in Hazelwood (the village goes on without you)

**What to build:** Build the lived-in world as a vertical slice: **one year in Hazelwood, season by season**, with named people doing things whether or not the player is watching. Deliver: (a) **the place**: a map in words (the square, the bridge, the mill, the market, the lane to Moonedge, the manor road, the forest edge, the river and the barge landing, the far town Weir), distances in "how long a walk", who lives where; (b) **the village at work**: trades, who buys what, what the village needs from a farm and why a burnt-farm girl's food matters; (c) **foodways**: staple foods and festival foods linked to the game's real dishes (`recipes.json`); (d) **calendar**: festivals, market days, the Harvest Fair and dance, the share day, with what happens in the village each season of each chapter regardless of the player (an **almanac**); (e) **folk belief, sayings, songs, children's games, superstitions** (light pagan, no religion), at least 25 sayings and customs a six-year-old could repeat; (f) **gossip map**: who knows what about the Moonedge fire and what they believe; (g) **50+ named background people** (the 28 favour-givers re-cast included) with one life-line each and one thing they are doing off-screen; (h) the Lord's manor and its reach, the river trade, the weather and the land. Every item that could become play gets an event seed (charter §5), at least 30 in all; the rest is background the other slices can lean on.
