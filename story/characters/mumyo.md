## At a glance ✅ (from the parent's profile)

No bigger than a hand. Appears and disappears at will. Flies and hops about the forest. White skin, wild white hair, a long tail, tiny
insect wings, a black belly. **A tiny grey vest with bells that jingle when he moves.** Loves jokes, songs and games. Tries to trick
others into mischief, takes it too far, gets into trouble. Disruptive because he is lonely; craves closeness and sabotages it. His bells
give away what he feels. Secretly tends a garden of night-blooming flowers.

## In this game 💡 (D12)

- ✅ **Fenna never sees him again after the fire.** So he is in the game as **traces**: bells, pranks, moved things, flowers left at
  the door, the night garden.
- 💡 **The player sometimes sees him:** half-hidden in a story picture (behind a bucket, in the scarecrow's hat), never looking at the
  viewer. Fenna, in the same picture, looks the other way. A secret between the player and the story.
- 💡 **He has no voice in this game.** Only bells. That keeps the frame's line clean: *a little fellow with bells*.

## The bells are his language 💡

| Sound | Means |
|---|---|
| quick jingle-jangle | excited, a prank is coming |
| a single *tink* | sad, sorry |
| two soft rings | "I'm here" |
| silence | ashamed, hiding (after the fire) |

## The ladder

| Rung | Answer |
|---|---|
| 1 Want | For Fenna to laugh and play again, without her ever seeing him. 💡 |
| 2 Embarrassed want | To be forgiven. He would rather be blamed than ask. 💡 |
| 3 Spine | **Make something happen so somebody looks.** 💡 |
| 4 Wrong belief | **If she saw me, she would send me away.** (After the fire, he thinks this is proven.) 💡 |
| 5 Origin 🔒 | He came to a lonely child who had no time to play. He was her play. Then his play burned her home. |

**Need:** show himself and say sorry. **He does not manage it in this game.** 🔒 He hears her say what happened (ch 6), and her message
reaches him only through Minena, fifty years on, in *The Forest Within*. His arc closes outside this game; that is the hook.

## His arc in the game 💡 (pranks turn into gifts)

| Ch | Trace |
|---|---|
| 1 | Bells in the air. Three sticks in a very silly pile. Nothing lost. |
| 2 | The hammer on the roof corner. Weeds pulled into a smiley face. |
| 3 | The scarecrow wears Fenna's hat. A bell far too small for a cow, on a nail at the mill (the player sees whose). At the dance, the mugs float, one falls, it's caught; a single *tink*. |
| 4 | He tries his tricks on the steward (Quill's ledger pages swapped into a paper boat). The steward is not amused. Fenna is, a little. |
| 5 | Night-flowers left on the doorstep, a few at a time. |
| 6 | The night garden. When she speaks, a single *tink*, then two soft rings. She does not know what they mean. The player does. |

## Rules

Pranks are harmless: moved, never lost; nobody hurt; nothing the player has is taken (the game never punishes). He never helps with work
(he cannot be made to work: her exact opposite).

## Inventions in this file 💡

No voice in the game; the bell language; the prank list; the player glimpses him; his arc closing in *The Forest Within*.
