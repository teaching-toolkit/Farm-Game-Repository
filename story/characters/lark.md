# Lark

Round 3, draft 1 (7 Oct 2026). ✅ magical, mythical, practically immortal (never an issue), modelled on **Wit** from the Stormlight
Archive. From the parent's fish story: rust-brown coat with mismatched buttons; "a bit of everything"; sorts books by weight; *"Probably fine."*

## At a glance 💡

- **Looks:** tall, thin, ageless (could be thirty, could be three hundred), rust-brown coat with mismatched buttons, always one more pocket
  than you counted. 💡 **No bells in this game**, so the bells stay Mumyo's clue. (Option: give him bells and use them as a *red herring* in
  chapters 1–2. Kids like red herrings, but it muddies the one clue the mystery hangs on. ❓)
- **Voice:** quick, sideways, fond of nonsense that turns out to be true. Insults that are really compliments, and the other way round.
  Never explains a joke.
- **Habits:** sorts things in absurd orders (books by weight, tools by mood); sits down when everyone else is busy; shows up with no
  sound of arriving.

## The ladder

| Rung | Answer |
|---|---|
| 1 Want | To see whether she will find it herself. |
| 2 Embarrassed want | To be asked to stay. Nobody asks a wanderer to stay. |
| 3 Spine | **Say the true thing as a joke, and never push.** |
| 4 Wrong belief | If I told her straight, it would break her. (Partly true, which is why he holds it.) |
| 5 Origin 🔒 | He has watched people told the truth too early, and wrecked by it. |

## What he does in the story 💡

He **never solves anything** and **never explains** (rule 6 in `../world/shell-and-magic.md`). He arrives when Fenna is most stuck,
sits down, says something ridiculous, and leaves. Later she realises it was the answer.

| Ch | Appearance |
|---|---|
| 2 | Turns up the day the market stall opens. Helps carry, stacks crates wrongly: "Probably fine." It is. Mostly. |
| 4 | Sits on the fence while the steward counts. "You've counted the cows. Have you counted the clover? No? It counts *you*." |
| 5 or 6 | Names the melody, as a joke, at the forest edge: "Whose tune is that? Yours, I'd say. Badly hummed." 🔒 (It is.) |
| 6 | The Fair: Fenna lays a place for him at the table without a word. He has to sit very still for a moment. (His want, given.) |

## Never

Bells (unless the parent chooses the red herring); solving; explaining; pushing; appearing more than about once a chapter.

## Inventions in this file 💡

Everything except what is marked ✅: no bells; the lines; the appearances; his embarrassed want.
