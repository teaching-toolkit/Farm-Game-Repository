# Berit and Fenna's family

Round 3, draft 1 (7 Oct 2026). Labels as elsewhere. Names marked 💡 are proposals.

## Berit, the grandmother who raised her 💡 (name)

- **Who:** Fenna's father's mother. Born at Moonedge (Y−62), saw all six Helpers at work as a child, started the farm book at 17. Was
  overruled by her son when the Lord's share rose. Took Fenna to a hill cottage after the fire ✅ and raised her there. Dies in the winter
  before the game, at 69.
- **In the game she is a voice, not a person:** her **handwriting in the book**. Margin notes, dry and short, sometimes funny, sometimes
  sharp. Fenna argues with them. They are the closest thing to Berit talking.
  - *Start small. Small is plenty.* (page one)
  - *Goats lie. —B.*
  - *Rest the field. Rest yourself. —B.*
  - *Do not argue with a tortoise. You will lose, slowly.*
  - one page torn out (the badger's; found in the still-room, chapter 5)
- **Spine:** keep the old ways. **Wrong belief:** if I had shouted louder, they would have stayed. 🔒 **Never said:** she blamed herself,
  not the child.
- **What she did not teach Fenna:** how to stop. She raised her on chores, because chores were the only thing that calmed the child. 🔒
- 🔒 She never wrote the rhyme down; she sang it. Fenna remembers it line by line.

## Fenna's parents 💡

- **Father:** tired, decent, not a villain. He said yes to the Lord because he believed next year would be easier. In the town he drives
  a carter's wagon. He cannot talk about Moonedge. 🔒 He is the one who most needs to see it alive.
- **Mother:** practical and loving, worried. 💡 **Her letters** come by the river barge (Captain Mei, a favour-giver) about once a chapter:
  first *come home before the frost*, later *stay if you must*, and at the end *we're coming to the Fair*. They carry the family strand
  and the early clock without any extra mechanic.
- **Younger siblings:** two (about 5 and 2 at the fire). They barely remember the farm. ❓ names. In chapter 6 they run straight to the animals.

## The older cousin ❓ (flexible, leaning yes)

- **Who:** the daughter or son of Fenna's father's sister, who also lived at Moonedge. Twelve at the fire, about nineteen now; lives in the
  town with Fenna's parents. ❓ name.
- **Spine:** somebody must be to blame. **Want:** for the fire not to have been nobody's fault. **Wrong belief:** if I find who did it,
  it will stop hurting.
- 🔒 **What the cousin saw:** Fenna running out of the barn, sooty, crying "the lights!". It was true. The cousin is not lying; they are
  only wrong about what it meant.
- **In the story:** mostly offstage, in Fenna's head and in the mother's letters ("your cousin asks after you" , which Fenna reads as a
  threat). 💡 Comes with the family to the Fair in chapter 6. Fenna's bend (saying aloud what happened) could be **said to the cousin**.
  That makes the risk real and gives the cousin a bend too: listening.

## Inventions in this file 💡

Berit's margin notes; her guilt; the parents' jobs; the letters; the cousin's age, what they saw, and the Fair.
