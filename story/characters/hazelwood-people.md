# Hazelwood people (the farmer's time)

Round 3, draft 1 (7 Oct 2026). Labels as elsewhere. Remember: Minena's grown-ups are **not born yet**; their **family shops** are here, and ✅ **the family names stay the same** (Appleby,
Odinson, Solemate, Greenstem …) for continuity with *The Forest Within*.
The farmer's contemporaries are old in Minena's time.

## The recurring few 💡

| Who | Spine | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Young Greenstem** (the farmer's age) | Know the name of everything | Minds his gran's herb stall at the market. **The first person who remembers her** (ch 2): "You're the Moonedge girl. You used to carry a bucket bigger than you." Asks too many questions. Learns from Wren and the badger's remedies in chapter 5 and becomes, much later, Minena's Mr. Greenstem. His later line, *the most important things are the ones we can't see*, he learned here. |
| **Granny Maud** (favour, ch 1) | Feed everyone | **Berit's oldest friend.** Knew the farmer as a baby. The first warm door in Hazelwood. Her goat keeps running off (and the goat is *not* Bramble, which disappoints everyone). |
| **The miller** (❓ name; Ella's father) | Keep the wheel turning | **Still blames Moonedge:** the silted stream nearly stopped his wheel. Gruff with the farmer in chapters 1–2. In chapter 3, after the pond is dug out and the stream runs clear again, he stands on the bridge watching it, and nods at her once. That is his whole apology. |
| **Ella** (favour, ch 1) | Be friends with everyone | The miller's daughter, a bit younger than the farmer; caught between her father and the new girl. |
| **Wren the healer** (favours, ch 2 and later) | Get the dose right | ✅ A woman: the herb woman from the next valley, and ✅ **young Greenstem's mentor** (he becomes her apprentice in chapter 5). Runs the plant check in the medicine chapter; grumpy at your bedside if you skipped it. Recognises Berit's still-room for what it was. Grown-up, plain-spoken. The human side of the medicine chapter; the badger is the magical side. |
| **The Odinson smithy** | – | Old smith Odinson and **Bo**, his son and apprentice (favour, ch 3). 💡 Bo is Minena's Mr. Odinson's father. |
| **The Appleby bakery** | – | Kept by **Mistress Appleby** (grandmother of Minena's Mrs. Appleby). Buys flour; first to sell the farmer's bread. |
| **The Solemate cobbler's** | – | Fixes the farmer's boots, more than once (a family tradition, it turns out). |
| **Master Quill**, the Lord's steward | Get the numbers right | Arrives in chapter 4 ✅. Stiff, fair, a ledger under his arm. Counts everything. Is surprised by what he finds: a farm that gives back more than it takes. 🔒 Writes the true numbers to the Lord. |
| **The Lord** (❓ name) | More | Offstage. A name on a seal. 🔒 Afraid of the Count downriver. |
| **Pieter the night watchman** (ch 3 on) | Carry the Lord's book | Collects the share; kind, tired, sure he is the villain of everyone's day. Lantern in daylight, the flask 💡, the receipt that says "Thank you" 💡. Sees the tiny light (ch 4). |
| **Captain Mei** (favour) | Keep moving | Runs a river barge between Hazelwood and the town. **Carries the mother's letters** (💡). |
| **Otto the ferryman** (favour, ch 2) | – | Ferried the family away the night they left. Remembers the child who did not cry. |
| **Old Jan** (favour, ch 2) | – | Was a farmhand at Moonedge long ago. "That goat could count, you know." Nobody believes him. |

the miller's grudge and nod, Wren as the herb woman, Bo as an Odinson, Mistress Appleby, Captain Mei and the letters, the two favours moved to chapter 5.
