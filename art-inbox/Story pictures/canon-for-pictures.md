# Canon for story pictures: what a picture may and may not show

Context file for the Farm Quiz Game (Hazelwood Harvest). Written 14 Oct 2026 with the picture review. Use it as the checklist for every
story picture, old or new. Labels: ✅ decided by the parent · 💡 proposed (Claude or the review), not yet confirmed · ❓ open.

Sources (in the project folder): `story/book-file.md`, `story/characters/*.md`, `story/world/*.md`, `story/story-delivery.md` §5,
`planning/2026-10-09-parent-notes.md` ([#S5], [#S6]), `TODO.md` §5 (R3), `00-READ-ME-FIRST.md` §4.6, `story/texts/ch1.md` … `ch6.md`.

## 1. The farmer (the player's character)

- ✅ **The farmer is never painted.** The player's own look is not fixed (gender and looks are open), so the farmer is never shown face-on.
- Allowed, and only these:
  - a **hand reaching in from the camera** (work mitts, holding a thing, reaching for a grain),
  - a **backlit silhouette from behind, in a farmer's hat**, the light coming from the front (✅ [#S6]),
  - the **view through the farmer's eyes** (no body in the picture at all).
- Village-square scenes and visits may show avatars drawn by the game in the player's own look (✅ R3). Those are made by the game, not by OpenArt.
- The name in the text is `{player}`; no gendered word for the farmer.

## 2. Mumyo (the little fellow with bells)

- ✅ **Traces only** in the main story. He is no bigger than a hand, appears and disappears, and is seen by the player only as traces: bells,
  a moved thing, a flower left on a step, a tail.
- His look (from `characters/mumyo.md`): white skin, wild white hair, a long tail, tiny insect wings, a black belly, a tiny grey vest with bells.
- ✅ **Fenna never sees him again after the fire.** So in any picture where he is, he must not look at the viewer and the farmer must not look at him.
- 💡 The player may see him **half-hidden** in a story picture (behind a bucket, in the scarecrow's hat), never looking at the viewer. The farmer
  in the same picture looks the other way.
- He has no voice. Only bells.
- Current pictures that show him: the thistle pile (tail and bell in the heap), the bell in the old mill, the scarecrow's hat, the tent with the bell,
  the reeds with the bells. A goat with a collar bell is a separate problem (§6). Check each one against this section.

## 3. The Helpers (animals and the pets)

- ✅ **The Helpers do not talk.** No speech bubbles, no talking faces, no sitting round a table reacting like people. Show what they do; the text says
  what it means (the goat looks at your list, then eats it).
- Helpers are animals: bunny, goat, alpaca, badger, tortoise, hens, horse, cow, squirrel, the farm cat. The badger's tea picture is open:
  the text puts him at the still-room door, the picture shows a kitchen (see the review, `c5_badger`).
- The squirrel is an ordinary squirrel. ✅ (14 Oct 2026, parent) **It may sit on Lark's shoulder at the Fair supper.** Its naming ("Nutmeg, obviously")
  stays in the free-play piece `sq_free_lark`; the supper picture does not need to show a name.
- The magpie stays out of the shell (✅ `book-file.md`, the forest rule). Do not draw a magpie in any story picture.
- Crows, owls, robins and other birds are ordinary birds (review, 14 Oct 2026).

## 5. Places, objects and the fire

- ✅ **No church, chapel, priest or religion.** The bell belongs to the village hall. No crosses, no altars.
- ✅ **The fire began low, in the hay corner of the barn.** The scorch marks start low. This is the clue (ch2 "Where the fire began").
- ✅ **The Lord's seal** is a red wax seal. The **stag on the seal is not canon** (review): do not reuse it unless the parent accepts it.
- 💡 **The mill:** a windmill with sails is in five pictures (the ch3 bell ruin, the ch6 sails, the three end-of-story views). A water wheel is in one
  (the miller-bridge picture) and in the lease document. **Undecided.** The review recommends the windmill. See the handoff.
- 💡 **The conch (white spiral shell):** in the forest it is small and far off, and it does not move (the text says so). In Minena's hands at the end
  of the story (frame 6) it is not in the text; the review suggests removing it. ❓ parent's decision.
- The night garden (white flowers, fireflies, the milk bowl): no Mumyo in the picture; two small bells on the reeds or by the bowl are fine.
- Winter: bare trees and snow are fine; snow on the roofs is shown by the game's shader, so pictures do not need snow caps baked in.

## 6. Animals and bells

- 💡 **No visible collar bells on any animal in a story picture.** The bells are Mumyo's clue; a bell on every goat and alpaca blurs it. The goat
  on the thistle field, the alpaca at the gate and the goat at the Fair supper all wear one now. (Review proposal, 14 Oct 2026; the parent confirms.)
- The bells that are allowed: Mumyo's bell (in a hidden place), the village hall bell (in the village), the small bell on the mill beam and the
  bells on the reeds in the night garden.

## 7. Words, letters and frames

- ✅ **No words on the map and no legible text in a picture** (`00-READ-ME-FIRST.md` §4.6, applied to story pictures). Letters, books and notices show
  handwriting as scribble or as shapes. Never a real word, never a name, never "Lorem ipsum".
- Letters in pictures: the letter from home says nothing that could be read. In particular **no "Clara"** (no such name in the story files).
- Frame pictures (the grandchildren at the bedside): the lamp, the stone walls, the quilt, the tea. Nobody is in the bed.
- The farm book (the in-game book beat, `c1_book`): a sketch of a rabbit in clover, no children and no legible words.

## 8. Picture format

- ✅ Hand-painted storybook look: soft painterly shading, warm earthy colours with olive greens and warm browns, a clean dark-brown outline (the
  style block in the OpenArt prompts; `openart_metadata.csv` has every prompt).
- 💡 **Full-bleed**: the picture fills its whole frame. No white or cream border, no vignette that fades to paper at the edges. (The picture
  brief used in the review; the parent to confirm it as a rule.)
- Single scenes, not sprite sheets. The sheet rule (three versions per sheet) is for game sprites; story pictures need their own rule (open).

## 9. Quick checklist for a new or changed picture

1. Is the farmer absent, a hand from the camera, or a backlit silhouette from behind? (§1)
2. Does Mumyo appear, if at all, as a trace or half-hidden, not looking at the viewer? (§2)
3. Are the Helpers silent and animal? No bells on animals? (§3, §6)
4. Are Lark, Greenstem (adult), Wren (a woman), the cousin (a girl), Quill (a steward) drawn as in §4?
5. No church or religious object, no Lord on the page, no legible words, no "Clara"? (§5, §7)
6. Full-bleed, no border? (§8)
7. Does the picture show what the piece's text says and nothing the text contradicts? (`story/texts/ch*.md`; the piece's id is in
   `story-picture-briefs.md`)
