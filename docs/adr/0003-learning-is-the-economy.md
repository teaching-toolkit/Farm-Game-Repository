# Learning is the economy: the Time Quiz moves time, Rest gives energy

Farm time moves only by **right first answers** in the Time Quiz (one question = one step); after a wrong first try the child keeps
trying and sees the explanation, but time does not move, and the question returns after the next one. Energy comes mostly from Rest
(mental arithmetic); the Time Quiz may also give or drain a little. Pets act on Time Quiz steps too, so automation never replaces
learning. Coins buy things but never knowledge alone: a card costs coins, reading time and showing the knowledge in a quiz; there is no
second currency. (`archive/log-2026-10.md` §2.4, §2.7, §2.8.)
