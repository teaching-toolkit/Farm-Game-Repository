# Natural consequences, never permanent loss

Over-use and neglect have natural consequences that last a while and can always be corrected; nothing is lost for good and Helpers
never leave. This replaced the earlier rule "nothing punishes the child" (10–12 Oct 2026). The one deliberate exception is the
poisoning in chapter 5: skipping Wren's plant check and using a wrong plant costs all coins and doubles energy costs for a few
questions. (`archive/log-2026-10.md` §2.7–2.8.)
